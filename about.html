Who we are

A local name you've trusted for 33 years

Chaparral Disposal Service LLC was built on a simple idea: your neighbors deserve dependable, affordable waste service delivered with real care. For more than three decades, the Gaytan family has done exactly that — showing up on time, treating customers right, and keeping our corner of New Mexico clean.

We're not a faceless national chain. When you call, you reach people who live here, know these routes, and take pride in the work. That's the difference three generations of local ownership makes.
