Frequently asked questions

What areas do you service?

We proudly serve Chaparral, La Mesa, Anthony, Berino, Mesquite, Vado, Chamberino, La Union, and San Miguel, New Mexico. Not sure if we reach you? Just give us a call.

How do I start residential trash service?

Reach out through our contact form or call (575) 824-0887. We'll confirm your address, set your route day, and get you started — usually within a few days.

What dumpster sizes do you offer?

We offer flexible dumpster options for businesses and job sites of all sizes. Tell us about your project and we'll recommend the right container and pickup schedule.

How do I pay my bill?

You can pay online quickly and securely through our Pay My Bill page, or contact our billing team at (915) 246-4689.

What are your business hours?

Our office is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM. You can reach us by phone during those hours, or send a message anytime through the contact form.
